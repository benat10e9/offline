# ADR 0009: Content factory is a parallel track from day one

Status: Accepted (2026-11-05)

## Decision

Acquisition, rights clearance, cleaning, classification, supersession tracking and validation of content is its own workstream, run in parallel with the runtime. First target: 5-10 GB of authoritative, rights-cleared, versioned text, not raw volume.

## Why

Content quality and rights, not code, are the product's main risk and moat.
