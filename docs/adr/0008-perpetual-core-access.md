# ADR 0008: Installed core content stays usable forever

Status: Accepted (2026-11-05)

## Decision

Entitlements gate updates, premium and agency packages, and commercial features. They never revoke access to content already installed. The core survival library remains usable if an entitlement lapses or the company ceases to exist.

## Why

Survival use must not depend on licensing state; government buyers will require it.
